Codex Quota implements these interactions with its existing Vue, VueUse and
shadcn-vue components. No React components or additional dependencies are copied.
Usage records and cost calculation retain the application's existing semantics.
The read-only title projection follows the upstream distinction between thread
titles and first-message content, including openai/codex issue #29007. SQLite
access uses rusqlite; its license is included in dependencies-LICENSES.txt.
